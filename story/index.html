Story 01 · Chicago

The Seal on the Refrigerator冰箱上的那枚福文

Maya, 34 · night-shift nurse
福星高照
Blessings of the Auspicious Star
For the ones who keep going · 致仍在坚持的人

Maya works nights. The hospital is quiet at 3 a.m., the kind of quiet that lets you think about rent, and the daughter asleep two hours away. She stuck the seal on the fridge — not on an altar, not in a frame. Just at eye level, where she reaches for the milk.

玛雅上夜班。凌晨三点的医院很静，静到会想起房租，想起两小时外熟睡的女儿。她把那枚福文贴在冰箱上——不是供起来，也不是装裱，就在伸手拿牛奶时看得见的地方。

"I don't think it changed my luck," she wrote to us. "But some mornings I'd catch sight of it and remember: I'm still here, and I'm still being looked after. That was enough to get through the shift."

"我不觉得它改了我的运气，"她给我们写道，"但有些早晨我瞥见它，会想起：我还在，而且还有人在照看我。这就够我撑完那一班了。"

A wish you keep close, not a promise from the sky. 一份贴身的祝愿，不是从天而降的承诺。

Story 02 · London

What His Father Left Behind父亲留下的那四个字

Daniel, 41 · architect
团圆美满
Reunion in Perfect Harmony
For the ones who miss someone · 致想念某个人的人

When Daniel cleared out his father's study, he found a small square of red paper folded into a book he'd never seen. Four characters in an old hand. He couldn't read them. He photographed it anyway, and only months later learned what they said.

丹尼尔清理父亲的书房时，在一本从没见过的书里，发现一张折着的红色方纸。四个字，是老人家的笔迹。他不认得，但还是拍了下来。几个月后他才知道那是什么意思。

团圆美满. Reunion in perfect harmony. "He didn't say it much," Daniel wrote. "But he kept it for thirty years. So I ordered the same four characters and put it on my desk. Now I say it for him, every day, without having to mean it out loud."

团圆美满。"他不常说这话，"丹尼尔写道，"但他收了三十年。所以我请了同样四个字放在桌上。现在我替他每天说一遍，不必出声。"

Some words are kept longer than they are spoken. 有些话，收藏得比说出口更久。

Story 03 · Toronto

The One Behind the Mirror镜子背后的那一枚

Sophie, 28 · illustrator
心想事成
All That You Wish Comes True
For the ones starting over · 致重新开始的人

After the move, Sophie taped 心想事成 behind the bathroom mirror. Not where guests would see it — where she would, brushing her teeth on the mornings she didn't want to get up.

搬家后，苏菲把「心想事成」贴在浴室镜子背后。不是给客人看的——是给她自己看的，在那些不想起床的早晨，刷牙的时候。

"It's not magic," she said. "It's just four characters asking me what I actually want. Most days I answer. Some days I don't. Either way, I look up."

"它不是魔法，"她说，"只是四个字在问我：你到底想要什么。大多数日子我会回答，有些日子不会。但无论如何，我抬起了头。"

The right four characters don't give you the answer. They ask you the question. 对的四个字不给你答案，它替你问出问题。
