鸾凤和鸣

Phoenixes Sing in Harmony

鸾凤和鸣，古人把两种鸟配在一起——凤是雌，雄叫凰。但我最喜欢的是那个"鸣"字。它是声音，是你们还愿意吵一架、说一句、笑一声。这张卡是为"还愿意说话"的爱情准备的。

Phoenixes Sing in Harmony — the ancients paired two birds. But I love the word sing. It is sound, it is you still willing to fight, to say something, to laugh. This seal, for the love that still talks.
