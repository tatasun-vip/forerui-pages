Love Deep as the Sea

情深似海，海是最不靠谱的比喻。海有潮汐、有风暴、有退潮。但反过来想，潮汐风暴退潮都是海。爱情也是这样——深不是不变化，是变来变去还是那个人。

Love Deep as the Sea — sea is the worst metaphor. Sea has tides, storms, ebb. But think the other way. Tide, storm, ebb, all still the sea. Love is the same. Deep is not unchanging. It is changing and still that person.
