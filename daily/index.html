燕侣莺俦

Swallow and Oriole Pair

燕侣莺俦，字面是好鸟成双。我倒觉得是说那种轻的、不累的陪伴。燕子黄莺都不重，飞飞停停，叽叽喳喳。这一张印的，是那种"在不在都行，但见了就好"的关系。

Swallow and Oriole Pair — the words mean good birds in pairs. I think it means the light, un-tiring kind of company. Swallows and orioles are not heavy. They fly, they stop, they chirp. This seal, for the kind of relationship where seeing each other is enough.
