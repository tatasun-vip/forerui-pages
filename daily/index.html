海誓山盟，誓言最不值钱。但你发现没，最不信誓言的人，最爱发誓。爱情里真正有分量的不是那一句"我愿意"，是后面十年你每天起来还是选择这个人。这张卡放在床下，是给后面那十年的。

Vows as Vast as Sea and Mountain — vows are cheap. But notice, those who trust vows least, swear the most. In love, what carries weight is not the I do, but the next ten years, every morning, choosing that person again. This seal under the bed, for those ten years.
